Accessibility

Fused Music Studio

Fused Music Studio offers specialist artistic collaboration, accessible innovation and training in music technology for disabled musicians, educators, and therapists. Drawing on 25+ years of experience in music education, commercial music, research and engineering, we work directly with disabled musicians to configure accessible setups to enable access to music making and teach music using accessible equipment. This includes using eyegaze-controlled instruments, adapted music software, switch-based hardware customisation and custom software design. We also train others to use accessible technology and work with schools, therapists, families to ensure that learning about accessible music making is also accessible to facilitators. Our mission is to tailor bespoke setups for the individual so that everyone can practise, create, perform, and enjoy music.

The Studio

Who we are

Dr Rebecca Colclasure
Founder

25+ years pedagogical experience alongside music engineering and accessible technology. Rebecca holds a PGCE, MA in music education, is a neurological music therapy professional associate, and recently completed her PhD in neuro rehabilitation, adapting open-source dance-based rhythm games for upper limb rehabilitation after stroke. Rebecca is a musician with specialism in co-design, music production and engineering, and bespoke music technology configuration.

Josh Saunders
Studio Partner

20 years in digital music marketing and innovation. Josh has produced award-winning fan activations and digital campaigns for Gorillaz, Dua Lipa, Ed Sheeran, Coldplay, David Bowie, Iron Maiden, and Pink Floyd. Judge for The Webbys and The Lovies Awards. Josh is also a bass player and guitarist who can be found playing at the Fused Music Studio live sessions.

Dr Richard Bennett
Collaborator (DJ HOTSOX)

Biochemist who has Locked-in Syndrome following a stroke at aged 50. Composes and performs music using eyegaze technology with custom drum machines and eyegaze overlays designed by Fused Music. An accomplished musician with a vast record collection who specialises in industrial German-inspired electronic music.
