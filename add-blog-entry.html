Add a Blog Entry

Fill in today's update, load your current blog.html, and this downloads a ready-to-upload replacement — no code editing required.

1Load your current blog.html

Download your current live blog.html from your host first (or use the copy already on your computer), then select it here.

2Write today's entry

Photos get embedded directly into blog.html, so no separate upload step — but large/many photos make the file bigger. A few phone photos are fine; avoid dozens of full-res images.

3Generate & download

This inserts your entry at the top of the journal and downloads the complete, updated blog.html.

Done! The updated blog.html just downloaded to your computer. Upload that file to your host, replacing the old one — that's the only manual step left.
