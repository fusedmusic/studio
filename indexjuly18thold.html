Accessibility

Fused Music Studio

Drawing on 25+ years of experience in music education, commercial music, research and engineering, we configure accessible setups to enable access to all for music making. We create interfaces and artefacts and non-proprietary solutions, explore neurofeedback and music, employ neurological music therapy techniques, and can teach music using accessible equipment. We can work very simply with you to configure your existing equipment or make suggestions on how you can make music using accessible music technology. We use eyegaze-controlled instruments, adapted music software, switch-based hardware customisation and custom software design. We offer support and training to use accessible technology for all sectors, and can also offer advice and point you in the direction of other facilitators, software developers and providers in this space!

The Studio

Who we are

Dr Rebecca Colclasure
Founder

25+ years pedagogical experience alongside music engineering and accessible technology. Rebecca holds a PGCE, MA in music education, is a neurological music therapy professional associate, and recently completed her PhD in neuro rehabilitation, adapting open-source dance-based rhythm games for upper limb rehabilitation after stroke. Rebecca is a musician with specialism in co-design, music production and engineering, and bespoke music technology configuration.

Josh Saunders
Studio Partner

20 years in digital music marketing and innovation. Josh has produced award-winning fan activations and digital campaigns for Gorillaz, Dua Lipa, Ed Sheeran, Coldplay, David Bowie, Iron Maiden, and Pink Floyd. Judge for The Webbys and The Lovies Awards. Josh is also a bass player and guitarist who can be found playing at the Fused Music Studio live sessions.

Dr Richard Bennett
Collaborator (DJ HOTSOX)

Biochemist who has Locked-in Syndrome following a stroke at aged 50. Richard composes and performs music using eyegaze technology using in-browser drum machines and eyegaze overlays and custom instruments designed by Fused Music. Richard is an accomplished musician and an aficionado in industrial German-inspired electronic music.
