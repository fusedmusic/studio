Fused Music Studio

We are an innovative research and design studio creating bespoke solutions for disabled people. We understand the wide-reaching scope and impact of accessible music-making, drawing on over 25 years of experience in music education and music engineering, and a wealth of knowledge from specialist accessible software developers and technology makers. We work directly with disabled children and adults, teachers, carers, and parents and train others to create accessible ways to ensure music can be enjoyed by everyone.

What We Offer

Ways we can work together

View all services
01

One-to-One Music Sessions

Personalised sessions focused on music making and designing a bespoke set up to achieve individual goals. Recent examples include enabling a school student to 'sing' using a bespoke eyegaze-controlled sample instrument, so she could sing karaoke and jam with her music therapist. Sessions can be ongoing or delivered as one off project.
02

In-Person Training

Practical in-person training for SEN departments and specialist schools — tailored to train small groups or individuals on existing access technology such as Soundbeam, or exploring how to adapt software and your existing set-up to your clients or students.
03

NHS & Neurorehabilitation Consultancy

Specialist consultancy integrating music technology into therapeutic practice for NHS and neurorehabilitation teams. Can focus on adaptation, tailoring, and co-design — drawing on stroke rehabilitation, music, and neurological music therapy approaches.
04

CPD for Music Therapists

Continuing professional development for qualified music therapists who want to expand their use of assistive technology.

Working with us

All of our work is bespoke — shaped around the individual, the setting, and the goals. Fees vary depending on the nature and scope of the work, and we're always happy to discuss what's possible within your budget.

We work with individuals, families, schools, NHS teams, and organisations — and we understand that funding routes and budgets differ widely across these contexts.
