About Rebecca

Where music technology meets inclusion

Rebecca brings over 25 years of experience in music education, accessible music technology, and music production — working across SEN schools, the NHS, and specialist organisations to ensure that music genuinely is for everyone.

Her PhD research used co-design with stroke survivors to adapt a rhythm game for upper limb rehabilitation — bringing together creative technology, clinical rigour, and lived experience in a way that defines her practice.
