About

Fused Music Studio

My name is Rebecca. I have spent over 25 years working within music, education, technology, and disability, building tailored solutions that open up music-making for disabled children, adults, and developing rehabilitation protocols and strategies with and for stroke survivors.

My background & approach

I'm a trained musician specialising in music technology, engineering and music production. From early in my studies and career I was intrigued by what creative technology could do — the possibilities within MIDI, controllers, external interfaces and DAWs for music composition and production.

After my degree and subsequently my teacher training, I taught in different schools and worked as training manager for the Drake Music Project. I drew on that experience to start building tailored solutions for disabled pupils who needed alternative ways to access music creation and production — working with accessible technology and equipment including Soundbeam, MIDI Creator, Apollo Ensemble and E-Scape.

My PhD brought a new research angle, adding to the individual tailoring I had been doing in practice for years. This research investigated whether a free and open source music gaming system could be modified to facilitate upper limb rehabilitation after stroke — involving intensive study of rehabilitation protocols, the stroke pathway, motivation, and developing creative resources including bespoke compositions and gaming levels for an online platform.

I now work as a researcher, rehabilitation specialist, teacher and accessible music consultant for individual clients, schools, NHS teams, and organisations including the British Society of Music Therapy, the Royal Hospital for Neuro-Disability, and DEMELZA.

My experience and research is centred around tailoring interventions and solutions to the individual — innovation and creative exploration using modern and legacy technology. Understanding how to adapt instruments and software is at the heart of everything I do, and what I aspire to teach others.

PhD Research

Rhythm games and stroke recovery

My doctoral research brought together two things I care deeply about: making technology genuinely useful to people after stroke, and involving those people in designing the tools they use.

Using co-design with stroke survivors, I adapted and modified a mainstream DDR rhythm game computer game for upper limb rehabilitation — a project that sits at the heart of what Fused Music Studio is about.
