Fused Music Studio

Fused Music Studio offers specialist artistic collaboration, accessible innovation and training in music technology for disabled musicians, educators, and therapists. Drawing on 25+ years of experience in music education, commercial music, research and engineering, we work directly with disabled musicians to configure accessible setups to enable access to music making and teach music using accessible equipment. This includes using eyegaze-controlled instruments, adapted music software, switch-based hardware customisation and custom software design. We also train others to use accessible technology and work with schools, therapists, families to ensure that learning about accessible music making is also accessible to facilitators. Our mission is to tailor bespoke setups for the individual so that everyone can practise, create, perform, and enjoy music.

The Studio

Who we are

Dr Rebecca Colclasure
Founder

25+ years in music education, music engineering, and accessible technology. Former Head of Music with a PhD in adapting open-source dance-based rhythm games for upper limb stroke rehabilitation. Specialist in co-design, music production and engineering, bespoke music technology configuration, and an avid musician working directly with disabled children, adults, and students.

Josh Saunders
Studio Partner

20 years in digital music marketing and innovation. Currently Senior Manager of Global Marketing Operations at Warner Music Group, previously Head of Firepit Technology and Senior Director of Innovation at Warner Music (2013-2023). He has produced award-winning fan activations and digital campaigns for Gorillaz, Dua Lipa, Ed Sheeran, Coldplay, David Bowie, Iron Maiden, and Pink Floyd. Judge for The Webbys and The Lovies Awards. As well as a long term collaborator, Josh is also an experienced bass and guitar player who can be found contributing to the Fused Music Studio live sessions.

Collaborator

Dr Richard Bennett

Dr Bennett (DJ HOTSOX) is a biochemist with Locked-in Syndrome and is a long term client of Fused Music Studio. Following a stroke when he was 50, Richard now lives at the Royal Hospital for Neuro-Disability. Richard composes and performs music using Eyegaze technology and eye controlled grid overlays for bespoke drum machines which have been designed specifically for his own requirements to control and perform music. Richard has a vast musical collection and is a particular aficionado of industrial German-inspired electronic music.
